# Music

`dark_corridor.ogg` ("The Dark Corridor") and `black_mirror.ogg` ("The Black Mirror")
are from the "Ancient Horrors" free pack by Atelier Magicae // Bunnixia.
Terms: free for commercial, non-commercial and personal use; do not redistribute
the files on their own. Credit is optional.

They play in the church once only 2 or 1 relics are left to find
(`_update_music` in scripts/main.gd, numbers in scripts/tuning.gd: MUSIC_*).
One of the two is picked at random each level; it plays until the level is finished.
